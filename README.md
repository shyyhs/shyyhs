### Hi there 👋

- 🔭 I'm an LLM engineer at [Preferred Networks](https://www.preferred.jp/en/), where I post-train [PLaMo](https://www.preferred.jp/ja/blog/tech/plamo-3-0-prime-release), our Japanese LLM built from scratch: reinforcement learning, long context, and agent harnesses.
- 🌱 Before that I was a researcher at [NICT](https://www.nict.go.jp/en/) (2023–2026). I received my Ph.D. in Informatics from Kyoto University in 2024.
- 💬 Ask me about multilingual, multicultural and multimodal LLMs, continual pre-training, and machine translation for low-resource languages.
- 📫 [shyyhs.github.io](https://shyyhs.github.io/) · haiyue.song.nlp@gmail.com

Here are some projects that I lead or contribute to:

- [OptiMer](https://github.com/nict-astrec-att/optimer): Optimal distribution vector merging for continual pre-training. Merging beats data mixing when adapting an LLM to new languages. EMNLP 2026 (Oral). [Project page](https://shyyhs.github.io/optimer/).
- [FormatRL](https://github.com/shyyhs/format-rl): Structured document translation via format reinforcement learning. IJCNLP-AACL 2025 (Oral).
- [CVQA](https://huggingface.co/datasets/afaji/cvqa): A culturally diverse multilingual visual question answering benchmark. I contributed half of the Japanese subset. NeurIPS 2024 Datasets and Benchmarks (Oral).
- [bytF](https://github.com/shyyhs/bytF): Byte-level n-gram F-scores for machine translation evaluation, now part of [sacreBLEU](https://github.com/prajdabre/sacrebleu). MT Summit 2025.
- [WAT25_IndicDoc](https://github.com/shyyhs/WAT25_IndicDoc): LLM baselines for the English–Indic document-level translation task I organized at WAT 2025.
- [NGLUEni](https://github.com/francois-meyer/nglueni): Benchmarking and adapting pretrained language models for Nguni languages. LREC-COLING 2024, Best Paper Award at AfricaNLP 2024.

Here are some earlier projects on neural machine translation:

- [CourseraParallelCorpusMining](https://github.com/shyyhs/CourseraParallelCorpusMining): Mining parallel lecture corpora from Coursera and multistage fine-tuning for lecture translation. LREC 2020.
- [eamt24-linguistic-mt](https://github.com/prajdabre/eamt24-linguistic-mt): Materials for our EAMT 2024 tutorial, Linguistically Motivated Neural Machine Translation.
- [NLPforCOVID-19](https://github.com/NLPforCOVID-19): A system for worldwide COVID-19 information aggregation. EMNLP 2020 NLP-COVID19 Workshop.
